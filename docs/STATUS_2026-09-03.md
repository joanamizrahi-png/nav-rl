## The finding of the day (18:30, corrected 18:50): the reward's footprint reads walkable ground where the cloud says grass

Measured along the actual eval trajectories, footprint by footprint, comparing
the CLOUD's label (SAM3 on the reconstruction) with the GENERATED label the
reward reads (traj field 4):

| pair | footprint steps where the cloud says grass | of those, generator said grass |
|---|---|---|
| warm | 65 | **0** |
| ancestor | 51 | **0** |
| cold | 4 | **0** |

So on this scene the reward is blind to the one terrain class the whole project
is about. This explains, at once: `grass = 0.000` in every `ground_share`;
blind ≈ sighted for both trained arms; warm ep5/ep14 reaching goals that sit on
22% / 41% grass without penalty; "goes on grass and does not crash".

**It is a semantics-model problem, not a policy one.** v26 epoch 10 is half
trained and the SANPO campus clips were never in its training set (a length
filter dropped them); v26b (seg loss active, 287 SANPO clips) is training now.
**What the generator painted instead (18:40):**

| semantics model | where the cloud says grass, the generator said | traversability score |
|---|---|---|
| v26 e10 (warm arm) | road 64, sidewalk 1 | 0.85 / 1.0 |
| v21 e12 (ancestor) | trail 40, pavement 11 | 0.85 / 1.0 |

Not vegetation — walkable classes, under both models, **at the footprint**.

**Correction (18:50, Joana):** the generator DOES draw grass — the eval video
frames show green on both flanks of the corridor. So "never draws grass" was an
overclaim. What is measured is a *boundary* disagreement: where the SAM3 cloud
labels call the verge grass, the generated semantics 1.5 m ahead of the robot
call it road/trail. Two readings fit, and the data does not yet separate them:
(a) the generator's grass boundary is tighter than the cloud's, so the verge
scores as pavement; (b) the cloud over-segments grass — SAM3 is not ground truth
(it measured 12x conservative against human labels elsewhere). Either way the
reward at the footprint did not see grass on those 65 / 51 steps, which is what
the policies experienced. The pinned goal-on-grass eval, 3 m into the flank
rather than at the verge, is the test that separates (a) from (b).

**Consequence for the paper:** the blind/sighted comparison on this scene is a
null by construction and must be reported as such, not as "vision does not
help". The goal-on-grass pinned eval (`GOAL_XY=10.0,-15.6`, spawn 64-68,
verified on the map: 83% grass under the goal) is the clean demonstration of
the failure mode, and the same eval re-run on v26b is the test of the fix.

## Findings worth keeping

* **Grass share is 0.000 for every policy on `gnd_AUw360`** even with grass a
  median 2.3 m from spawn. It is a real null, not an artifact. That scene's
  walkable corridor is labelled `road` by v26; there is no terrain decision.
  `gnd_AU_180` is the terrain scene (sidewalk 0.58, grass present).
* **Cold arm, paired timeouts: sighted ended closer than blind on every shared
  failure** (`closed_frac` −1.1/−1.1/−1.7 vs −3.3/−3.3/−3.3). Small, one seed,
  16k steps — but the first from-scratch vision signal.
* **The diffusion sampler runs `seed=0` every step** (`live_backend.py:222`).
  The world model's stochasticity is frozen. Say "deterministic given pose and
  action", not "stochastic world model".
* **Yaw ladder on `AU_180`**: collision fraction 0.62 where coverage < 0.2,
  0.026 where > 0.5 — the footprint reads reconstruction quality as much as
  ground. Coverage peaks at +108° off the walk direction, unexplained.
* **Coherence termination measures the WHOLE FRAME.** A well-rendered view over
  an unsupported ground patch does not trigger it. `void_terminate_frac` would;
  it is off everywhere, deliberately.
